# φ⁴ lattice configurations near the critical point

Monte Carlo configurations of the scalar φ⁴ theory on periodic lattices of volume V = L^D with action

$$S[\phi] = \sum_x \Big[-2\kappa \sum_{\mu=1}^{D} \phi_x \phi_{x+\hat\mu} + \phi_x^2 + \lambda\big(\phi_x^2-1\big)^2\Big]$$

| D | κ | λ | κ_c | L | configurations per L | total size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.2705 | 0.022 | 0.27088 | 16, 32, 64, 128 | 10240 | 1.8 GB |
| 3 | 0.1923 | 0.9 | 0.19225 | 16, 32, 64, 128 | 2560 | 49 GB |

κ_c is the critical point estimated from Binder-cumulant crossings in the reference below.
The folder DM/ holds the corresponding ensembles generated by the diffusion models of the
same reference.

Each array holds the float64 field values φ_x. Axis 0 runs over configurations in
Monte Carlo order and the remaining axes are the periodic lattice directions. The
configurations were generated with Fourier-accelerated HMC combined with Wolff cluster
updates. The 3D L = 128 file consists of ten independent chains of 256 configurations,
concatenated along axis 0.

## Autocorrelation

Every chain starts from a random field. One sampler iteration is one HMC trajectory
followed by Wolff cluster flips. τ_int is the integrated autocorrelation time of the
stored sequence in units of stored configurations, so 0.5 means consecutive
configurations are uncorrelated. M is the magnetisation and S the action of a
configuration, both defined in the next section.

| D | L | chains × configurations | warm-up iterations | stored every | HMC acceptance | τ_int(M) | τ_int(\|M\|) | τ_int(S) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 16 | 1 × 10240 | 2000 | 5th iteration | 0.77 | 0.52 | 0.53 | 0.56 |
| 2 | 32 | 1 × 10240 | 2000 | 5th iteration | 0.78 | 0.50 | 0.58 | 0.60 |
| 2 | 64 | 1 × 10240 | 2000 | 5th iteration | 0.78 | 0.49 | 0.64 | 0.66 |
| 2 | 128 | 1 × 10240 | 2000 | 5th iteration | 0.79 | 0.48 | 0.69 | 0.79 |
| 3 | 16 | 1 × 2560 | 2000 | 10th iteration | 0.77 | 0.55 | 0.60 | 0.55 |
| 3 | 32 | 1 × 2560 | 2000 | 10th iteration | 0.80 | 0.50 | 0.53 | 0.54 |
| 3 | 64 | 1 × 2560 | 2000 | 10th iteration | 0.75 | 0.48 | 0.78 | 0.88 |
| 3 | 128 | 10 × 256 | 2000 per chain | 10th iteration | 0.77 | 0.48 | 0.74 | 0.74 |

The slowest modes of this sampler are the Z₂-even global quantities S, the
nearest-neighbour correlator Σ_x Σ_μ φ_x φ_{x+μ̂}, Σ_x φ_x², M² and \|M\|, which relax
together. In sampler iterations their τ_int grows from about 1.3 (2D, L = 16) to 2.4
(2D, L = 128) and from 2.4 (3D, L = 16) to 4 to 7 (3D, L = 128), which sets the
thinning above. A scan over these quantities, M, Σ_x φ_x⁴, max_x \|φ_x\|, the mode
powers \|φ̃(k)\|² of the four lowest momentum shells and of k = (π, …, π) found no
stored observable with τ_int above τ_int(S) by more than 0.1.

## Observables

The magnetisation of a configuration and the quantities built from it are

$$M = \frac{1}{V}\sum_x \phi_x, \qquad \chi = V\big(\langle M^2\rangle - \langle|M|\rangle^2\big), \qquad U_L = 1 - \frac{\langle M^4\rangle}{3\langle M^2\rangle^2} .$$

The action S of a configuration is computed as

$$S[\phi] = \sum_x \Big[-2\kappa \sum_{\mu=1}^{D} \phi_x \phi_{x+\hat\mu} + (1-2\lambda)\phi_x^2 + \lambda\phi_x^4\Big],$$

which is the action at the top of this page without the field-independent constant
λV. Errors are jackknife over 64 blocks of consecutive configurations.

2D, κ = 0.2705, λ = 0.022

## Diffusion-model ensembles

The folder DM/ holds the diffusion-model ensembles of the reference below at the same
couplings. For each L a score network was trained on Monte Carlo configurations at that
L. Each configuration is an independent reverse-SDE trajectory, integrated with 2000
Euler–Maruyama steps on a logarithmic time grid in 2D and a linear time grid in 3D. The
arrays are float32 with the same axis order as the Monte Carlo files.

